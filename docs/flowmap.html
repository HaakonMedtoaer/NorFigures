NorFigures checks economic figures in a Norwegian text against SSB, Norges Bank and NAV. This is a replay of one real run. An AI model reads the text and says what each sentence claims; every official number comes from the sources, never from the model.

Inflasjonen var 3,1 % i oktober. Norges Bank holdt styringsrenten på 4,0 %, og analytikerne venter at kronen svekkes til 12,00 mot euro neste år.

In English: inflation was 3.1 % in October. Norges Bank held the policy rate at 4.0 %, and analysts expect the krone to weaken to 12.00 per euro next year.

The run plays by itself; press Restart to watch from the start, or Step to go one event at a time. Click a claim and then a box to see what went in and came out, including the exact query.
